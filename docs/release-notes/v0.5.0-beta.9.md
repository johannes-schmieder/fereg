Beta.9 is the second round of two-fixed-effect OLS Auto speed work on the beta.8 baseline. The aggregated (worker, firm) cells are now built on the worker team, bit for bit as the serial build, and before the diagonal pilot, so the pilot's Schur products use the cell operator; the serial cell kernel polls for Break per block of work. The pinned CMG crate dispatches a multigrid level's row-operator matvec to the worker team by its entries rather than its row count, so dense coarse levels with few rows no longer run serially. PPML Auto with two fixed effects runs the serial sections of its joint recurrence in exact parallel form and reuses a finished canonical probe.

On the audited Veneto panels on one Linux node, the candidate was faster than beta.8 in all twenty cells (0.87–0.99 of its native time) and faster than PyFixest `within` in nine of ten cells without saved effects. The fixed Linux qualification retained all observations: 123 of 126 protected comparisons satisfied the max(5%, 1 ms) bound under the documented accepted host-count exception and three were inconclusive by under a millisecond; the practical Mac check had 115 passes, eleven inconclusive comparisons and no regression. PPML exact timing passed all twelve campaign cells with identical science. Pilot low bits change within the certified tolerances; sample, rank, omission and every discrete decision are unchanged, and results stay independent of the worker count.

Includes Linux x86-64, Windows x86-64 and universal macOS binaries for Stata 18 or newer. Linux requires glibc 2.28 or newer. Native Intel Mac Stata testing remains outstanding; both macOS slices are included.

The corresponding-source ZIP includes the exact locked dependency sources, license texts, an offline Cargo configuration, and Mac build instructions.
