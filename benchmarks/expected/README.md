# Frozen benchmark expectations

Numerical and identity expectations are frozen by the OLS-v1 reference and
independent-oracle receipts. Candidate performance evidence is accepted only
after scientific parity and under the 0.5.0 gates in ADR-0007. Licensed baseline
captures must store sample hashes, coefficient/VCE matrices, iteration counts,
deletion counts, wall time, peak RSS, Stata metadata, and exact plugin hashes
here. Never use a speed number without the corresponding scientific outputs.

The reproducible AKM development fixture is generated by
`benchmarks/generators/generate_akm.do`; Johannes's
`tests/manual/simple_AKM.do` remains an unchanged manual playground. The
parameterized generator records observations, workers, firms, periods,
mobility, assignment rule, seed, and dataset hash before timing.

`scripts/solver_development.py` validates
`benchmarks/manifests/ols_solver_development_v2.json` and runs the registered
`quick`, `matrix`, `milestone`, or `freeze` cadence. It probes the pinned
reghdfe/ftools/require and gtools/gregress identities, separates scientific
preflights from timing, reports cold/warm median and MAD, and captures existing
fereg routing/native diagnostics. Timed reghdfe and fereg outputs must match the
tight oracle; gregress remains diagnostic-only. Generated JSON is exploratory
and belongs under `benchmarks/reports/` or an external work directory, never in
the frozen expected/receipt surface without an explicit qualification step.

The current frozen development policy is direct MAP for one FE, an at-most
four-sweep MAP pilot for multiway designs, LSMR only when pilot contraction is
at least 0.008 and total FE coefficients do not exceed 65,536, and genuine CG
otherwise. Auto-selected LSMR has one visible certified-CG fallback. CG must
meet both the comparator-equivalent Hestenes energy stop and the independent
FE certificate.

The local plugin checkpoint recorded in `STATUS.md` completed the full 100k
matrix with 148 successful timing records and only the two registered
fail-closed high-mobility IT diagnostics. Its selected 1m acceptance set passed
24/24 timed arms and 8/8 preflights; its selected 10m freeze passed 12/12 timed
arms and 4/4 preflights with five warm runs. These are mutable development
results until the exact source, plugin, dependency closure, and archive are
materialized as an immutable qualification receipt.
