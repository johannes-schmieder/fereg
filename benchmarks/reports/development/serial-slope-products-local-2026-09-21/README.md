# Serial saved-slope product experiment

This isolated draft retains computed coefficients for public serial, ordered,
full-rank single slope partitions with at most sixteen RHSs. The existing OLS
parent admits initial and combined products before retention; a smaller budget
keeps the nonretaining cache. Projection and fresh certificates retain their
arithmetic. Common output reconstruction and residual certificates still decide
acceptance. Explicit/shared, wide, shuffled, threaded and rank-deficient cases
keep their previous behavior.

The [nine-cell three-pair screen](timings.md) completes 78 calls with clean
isolation. All 27 native pairs have identical six-bundle numerical exports and
samples. Twenty-four saved-vector pairs pass the original tolerances; the
largest output difference is below 1.04e-11. Embedded independent output checks
pass. Ordinary fixest weights are excluded for frequency-weight inference.

Three-slope-only PW robust/clustered medians improve from 234/263 to 219/255 ms,
with paired native ratios 0.9316/0.9696. Their fixest medians remain 148/164 ms.
One-slope AW/FW and unit saved cases change little. Targeted tracked peaks rise
by 8,257,568–9,437,216 bytes; excluded controls have equal peaks and saved vectors.

Six diagnostic fits verify exact instrumented/uninstrumented exports and
outputs. Recovery falls from about 20 to 12 ms for AW and 33 to 20 ms for PW,
while storing/combining products adds roughly 5–9 ms earlier. Geometry
construction and fresh checks remain material costs. These diagnostic times
are not paired performance evidence.

The captured plugin passes its actual ABI fixture and three focused release
tests: retained-product reconstruction and budget boundary, weighted full-fit
MAP parity and scope controls, and existing exact fusion/certificate checks.
Initial compile mistakes and the expanded fixture's missing weight binding
remain preserved. Tests and evidence bind complete owf1, srp1 and profiling
sources. All 84 applications are archived; large arrays remain local with
hashes, and raw licensed logs are excluded.

The draft remains an experiment and is not adopted into the production
checkout. It establishes a modest PW gain with a memory tradeoff, not closure
of the serial weighted losses. Larger cycle-routing gains are investigated
separately; no fresh routing seed was used here.
