# One-FE membership reuse and bounded outcome scheduling

Two separately measured changes are retained. Narrow unit-weight IID fits
overlap their independent outcome mean/TSS scan with fitting when at least
eight workers and 16,384 rows are available, with at most 16 model columns.
The scan borrows immutable prepared data, owns no data buffer and retains its
original arithmetic and deferred error order.

One-FE unit-weight preparation can also construct the ordinary final membership
index first. When every group has at least two members, it reuses that index
as the singleton fixed-point witness and final topology owner. A singleton
causes the index to be dropped before generic pruning. Clustered, weighted,
small and keepsingletons calls retain the original route. Final topology
validation still independently checks every membership and singleton invariant.
The owner moves within the existing topology allowance; no second index or
observation-sized buffer is retained.
