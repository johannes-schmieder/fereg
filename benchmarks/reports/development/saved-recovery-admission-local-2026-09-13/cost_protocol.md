Run only after the exact-cap public rollback/oracle test, source overlap audit,
48-fit saved-output oracle and focused recovery/plugin tests pass. Compare the
existing unadopted saved-Schur plugin 18427c19 with the route-admitted prototype
247f889a. This isolates the budget extension; it is not adoption of saved Schur.

Use six fresh rotated pairs: exact original unit saved and PW partial saved
requests; weak robust, tiny weighted bridge and low-FW clustered controls from
the CG-repair archive; one small AW one-FE control with its saved FE requested.
Reuse each archived data/warm-up and validate imported hashes. No build, test,
allocation diagnostic or other benchmark overlaps these uninstrumented pairs.

No speedup is hypothesized. Require exact full b/V, sample/status/DoF,
certificates, saved outputs and non-time solver fields; record command/phase,
native/model/scratch and RSS values. Investigate an increase exceeding both 5%
and 10 ms with two additional same-specification seeds/rotated arms. Preserve
all failed/flagged observations. Do not broaden after the first passing screen.

The new recovery increments have scoped admission tests and source bounds;
the diagnostic confirms the original PW whole peak precedes recovery. The
public selected model still omits existing initial solver reservations. Report
that known discrepancy, rather than treating the public model as a whole-action
proof. This screen does not close P0/P6, establish original-baseline cumulative
speedups, or qualify every upstream factor allocation.
